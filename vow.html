Vows

I would like to first say thank you - thank you to everyone who could join us here today. Thank you for coming, it really means a lot to us. Thank you especially to our parents for whom we would not be here otherwise.

Kendall - I love you very much. I love you for everything that you are - beautiful, smart, funny. After all this time I believe I know you - I think that you're thinking funny should have been the first of those three adjectives. I've gotten to know you so well over these years and I've enjoyed spending time together. I enjoy you for you and I enjoy what we've become.

Our time together is a way to celebrate and embrace our idiosyncrasies. Kendall has this habit of leaving all the kitchen cabinets open for some reason, its not inherently bad or anything but so I've developed the habit of then closing all those cabinets whenever I go through the kitchen for a banana or something. I look forward to those stupid little things. It lets me know I understand you, and that we understand each other. I also look forward to all the time that we will spend together and everywhere we will go. All the places we have been together - I wouldnt want to spend with anyone else.

It's funny, last week my mom texted me a photo collection from her photos app of me and my mom together and like half of them I was wearing this Acadia sweatshirt. I got that sweatshirt on a trip one summer me and Kendall were living and working together in maine where we took a weekend and drove up to Acadia national park and I got that sweatshirt from a walmart and I wear it truthfully too often. To the point that I get made fun of for it and apparently wear too often that it shows up in way too many pictures.

But I wear it so much because it brings me comfort, because I love that trip so much - to me it defines a significant part of our relationship and I want to be reminded of it and enjoy those feelings all over again. It reminds me of you, and I like that.

I love you so much. I'm so excited for our lives together and to be there for you - and you be there for me. I'll happily follow you anywhere, especially if that means when I follow behind I'll be closing all the kitchen cabinets ... the kitchen drawers, the closet doors, the laundry room, the lid on your water bottle - that's an important one - as long as it means I'll be there with you.

I want to always be there for you. I'm so proud of how far we have come and am looking forward to the rest of the journey.
